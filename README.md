MSc-IT-Management

Welcome to my Master of Science in IT Management academic repository.

This repository documents my academic journey throughout the MSc in IT Management programme, including coursework, assignments, projects, research, practical exercises, and supporting materials.

The repository is organised into 12 units, covering fundamental computing concepts, software engineering, data science, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, risk management, strategic IT management, and emerging trends in computing.

🎓 About the Programme

Programme: Master of Science in IT Management

The programme combines technical computing knowledge with IT management, governance, risk management, strategic planning, and business alignment.

Through this programme, I am developing my understanding of how technology can be effectively managed, secured, governed, and aligned with organisational objectives.

📚 Units
Unit 1: What is Computing?

Introduction to computing, computer systems, computational thinking, and the role of computing in modern society.

Unit 2: Logical Foundations of Computing: Boolean Algebra, Gates, and Set Theory

Exploration of Boolean algebra, logic gates, set theory, and their applications in computing and digital systems.

Unit 3: Principles of Computer Science

Fundamental principles of computer science, algorithms, computational concepts, and problem-solving.

Unit 4: Software Engineering

Software development principles, software development methodologies, requirements, design, testing, maintenance, and software engineering practices.

Unit 5: Data Science and Storage

Data management, data science concepts, data storage, databases, data analysis, and the role of data in modern organisations.

Unit 6: Principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI) I

Introduction to artificial intelligence, fundamental AI concepts, intelligent systems, and applications of AI.

Unit 7: Principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI) II

Advanced AI concepts, applications, challenges, and the impact of artificial intelligence on organisations and society.

Unit 8: Principles of Cyber Security

Cybersecurity principles, threats, vulnerabilities, security controls, risk mitigation, and protection of information systems.

Unit 9: Computing and Risk

Computing-related risks, risk identification, assessment, mitigation, and the relationship between technology and organisational risk.

Unit 10: Strategic and Enterprise IT Management: Aligning IT with Business Goals

Strategic IT management, enterprise IT, business-IT alignment, technology strategy, and the role of IT in achieving organisational objectives.

Unit 11: Future-Proofing IT Systems: Integrating Risk Management and Strategic IT Governance

IT governance, strategic risk management, resilience, future-proofing technology systems, and integrating governance with organisational strategy.

Unit 12: Future Trends and Concepts in Computing

Emerging technologies, future trends in computing, technological innovation, and the potential impact of new technologies on organisations and society.
